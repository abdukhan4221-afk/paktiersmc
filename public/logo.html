EclipseMC Lifesteal identity

Every heart matters. One clear identity.

This guide captures the icy blue, bright green, soft neutrals, and rounded lettering in the supplied EclipseMC logo and banner. Use the palette and type consistently across the Lifesteal store, website, and community announcements.

Logo appearances

On light

Use a pale, low-texture surface.

On dark

Preferred for the Lifesteal website and dark theme.

On brand color

Keep clear space around the wordmark.

Brand color palette

Typography and usage

Display

Fredoka SemiBold

Rounded, dimensional, and optimistic.

Use for logo lettering, page headings, short feature titles, and major statistics. Avoid long paragraphs in this style.

Body

Nunito Sans Bold / Regular

Friendly geometric letterforms stay readable at small sizes. Use regular for body copy and semibold or extra-bold for labels, buttons, and navigation.

Keep

Protect the silhouette

  • Leave breathing room around the wordmark.
  • Keep blue and green in their original order.
  • Use subtle shadows rather than hard black outlines.
  • Choose uncluttered backdrops with strong contrast.
Avoid

Don’t dilute the brand

  • Do not stretch, skew, or recolor the wordmark.
  • Avoid neon colors outside the palette.
  • Do not place tiny text over detailed backgrounds.
  • Keep keyboard focus and contrast visible in UI.
